# TabArena provenance note

The empirical evaluation uses TabArena evidence with 51 tasks, 14 pre-specified methods, and 816 paired fold vectors. One disclosure is the complete method-loss vector for one recorded fold of one task.

Third-party raw TabArena artifacts are not redistributed here where redistribution rights are unclear. Reproduction should retrieve the upstream benchmark materials and apply the task set, method set, aggregation rule, normalized-midrank rule, task-weight neighborhood, and disclosure specification stated in the manuscript and supplement.
