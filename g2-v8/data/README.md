# Data provenance and redistribution note

The empirical study uses TabArena benchmark evidence.

Study scope reported in the manuscript:
- 51 tasks;
- 14 pre-specified methods;
- 816 paired fold vectors.

One disclosure is the complete method-loss vector for one recorded fold of one task.

Third-party raw TabArena artifacts are not redistributed in this folder where redistribution rights are unclear. Reproduction should retrieve the upstream TabArena materials and apply the task set, candidate set, score direction, fold aggregation, normalized-midrank rule, nominal weights, total-variation radius, and disclosure specification stated in the paper and supplement.

The public summaries in this repository are derived publication-facing records, not substitutes for the upstream benchmark archive.
